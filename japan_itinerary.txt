12-Day Japan Itinerary for Family with 10-Year-Old Video Game Lover

Day 1 (January 1st): Arrive in Tokyo
- Arrive at Narita International Airport
- Transfer to your hotel in central Tokyo
- Explore the Akihabara district, known as the "Electric Town" and hub of Japan's otaku (geek) culture
- Visit the Sega Arcade and try out the latest video games
- Dinner at a themed restaurant like the Mario Cafe & Store

Day 2: Tokyo
- Start your day at the Imperial Palace and East Gardens
- Visit the Sensoji Temple in Asakusa, one of Tokyo's most famous temples
- Explore the Shibuya Crossing, one of the world's busiest pedestrian crossings
- Spend the afternoon at the Nintendo Tokyo Store and Super Nintendo World at Universal Studios Japan

Day 3: Tokyo
- Take a day trip to Yokohama, a port city just south of Tokyo
- Visit the Yokohama Hakkeijima Sea Paradise, an aquarium and amusement park
- Ride the Ferris wheel and try out the various video game attractions
- Return to Tokyo in the evening and have dinner in Shinjuku

Day 4: Tokyo
- Explore the Ghibli Museum, dedicated to the famous anime studio
- Spend the afternoon at the Teamlab Borderless digital art museum
- Enjoy a panoramic view of Tokyo from the Tokyo Skytree observation deck

Day 5: Kyoto
- Take the shinkansen (bullet train) to Kyoto, the cultural heart of Japan
- Visit the iconic Kinkakuji (Golden Pavilion) and Kiyomizudera Temple
- Stroll through the Arashiyama bamboo grove
- Explore the Nijo Castle, a UNESCO World Heritage site

Day 6: Kyoto
- Start your day at the Fushimi Inari Shrine, famous for its thousands of orange torii gates
- Visit the Nijo Castle, a UNESCO World Heritage site
- Spend the afternoon in the Gion district, the geisha district of Kyoto
- Attend a traditional Japanese tea ceremony

Day 7: Kyoto
- Take a day trip to Nara, known for its friendly deer roaming the streets
- Visit the Todaiji Temple, home to the world's largest bronze Buddha statue
- Explore the Nara Deer Park and feed the deer
- Return to Kyoto in the evening

Day 8: Osaka
- Take the shinkansen to Osaka, Japan's third-largest city
- Visit the Osaka Castle, one of Japan's most famous castles
- Explore the Dotonbori district, known for its vibrant food and entertainment scene
- Try your hand at go-karting on the streets of Osaka, dressed as your favorite Mario Kart character

Day 9: Osaka
- Visit the Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan, one of the largest aquariums in the world
- Spend the afternoon at the Universal Studios Japan theme park
- Enjoy the various video game-themed attractions, including Super Nintendo World

Day 10: Hiroshima
- Take the shinkansen to Hiroshima, a city known for its tragic history and resilience
- Visit the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park and Museum
- Take a ferry to the nearby island of Miyajima, known for the iconic Itsukushima Shrine

Day 11: Hiroshima
- Explore the Hiroshima Castle and its museum
- Visit the Mazda Museum to learn about the history of the Mazda car company
- Spend the afternoon relaxing in the Shukkei-en Garden

Day 12 (January 13th): Depart Japan
- Spend the morning exploring any remaining sights in Hiroshima
- Transfer to the airport for your flight back to El Paso, Texas
