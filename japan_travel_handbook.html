12-Day Japan Itinerary for Family with 10-Year-Old Video Game Lover

This 12-day itinerary covers the top destinations in Japan, with a focus on video game-related attractions and activities suitable for a family with a 10-year-old child.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1 (January 1st): Arrive in Tokyo
    • Arrive at Narita International Airport
    • Transfer to your hotel in central Tokyo
    • Explore the Akihabara district, known as the "Electric Town" and hub of Japan's otaku (geek) culture
      • Visit the Sega Arcade and try out the latest video games
      • Check out the retro game stores and collectible shops
    • Dinner at a themed restaurant like the Mario Cafe & Store
  2. Day 2: Tokyo
    • Start your day at the Imperial Palace and East Gardens
    • Visit the Sensoji Temple in Asakusa, one of Tokyo's most famous temples
    • Explore the Shibuya Crossing, one of the world's busiest pedestrian crossings
    • Spend the afternoon at the Nintendo Tokyo Store and Super Nintendo World at Universal Studios Japan
      • Experience the latest Nintendo attractions and ride the Mario Kart ride
      • Browse the exclusive Nintendo merchandise and collectibles
  3. Day 3: Tokyo
    • Take a day trip to Yokohama, a port city just south of Tokyo
      • Visit the Yokohama Hakkeijima Sea Paradise, an aquarium and amusement park
      • Ride the Ferris wheel and try out the various video game attractions
    • Return to Tokyo in the evening and have dinner in Shinjuku
  4. Day 4: Tokyo
    • Explore the Ghibli Museum, dedicated to the famous anime studio
    • Spend the afternoon at the Teamlab Borderless digital art museum
    • Enjoy a panoramic view of Tokyo from the Tokyo Skytree observation deck
  5. Day 5: Kyoto
    • Take the shinkansen (bullet train) to Kyoto, the cultural heart of Japan
    • Visit the iconic Kinkakuji (Golden Pavilion) and Kiyomizudera Temple
    • Stroll through the Arashiyama bamboo grove
    • Explore the Nijo Castle, a UNESCO World Heritage site

Travel Tips

  • Familiarize yourself with the Japan Rail (JR) system for efficient transportation
  • Download a translation app or learn some basic Japanese phrases to communicate with locals
  • Be respectful of cultural customs and etiquette in Japan
  • Stay hydrated and try the local cuisine throughout your trip
  • Pack comfortable walking shoes and dress in layers to accommodate the weather
  • Bring snacks and activities to keep your 10-year-old entertained during long travel days
  • Research and book any video game-themed attractions or experiences in advance
  • Consider purchasing a Japan Rail Pass to save on transportation costs
  • Prepare for potential language barriers by downloading a translation app or learning some basic Japanese phrases
  • Familiarize your child with Japanese culture and customs before the trip to help them better understand and appreciate the experience
  • Pack light and utilize the luggage forwarding service to avoid carrying heavy bags during your travels
  • Incorporate some kid-friendly activities and attractions, such as the Yokohama Hakkeijima Sea Paradise, to keep your 10-year-old engaged and entertained
