Job Descriptions and Recruitment Plan for Manus AI Clone Development Team:

AI/ML Engineer:
- Responsibilities: Design and develop advanced machine learning models and algorithms for the Manus AI system
- Requirements: Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related field; 5+ years of experience in AI/ML development
- Salary Range: $120,000 - $180,000 per year

Computer Vision Specialist:
- Responsibilities: Implement state-of-the-art computer vision techniques for object detection, recognition, and tracking
- Requirements: Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science or Electrical Engineering; 3+ years of experience in computer vision
- Salary Range: $90,000 - $140,000 per year  

Robotics Engineer:
- Responsibilities: Design and integrate the hardware components, sensors, and actuators for the physical Manus AI system
- Requirements: Bachelor's or Master's in Robotics, Mechanical Engineering, or Electrical Engineering; 5+ years of experience in robotics
- Salary Range: $100,000 - $160,000 per year

Project Manager:
- Responsibilities: Oversee the overall development process, coordinate the team, and ensure the project stays on track
- Requirements: PMP certification or equivalent; 7+ years of experience managing complex software/hardware projects
- Salary Range: $110,000 - $170,000 per year

Recruitment Plan:
- Leverage professional networks and industry connections to identify qualified candidates
- Post job listings on relevant job boards and online communities
- Offer competitive compensation packages to attract top talent
- Implement a thorough screening and interview process to evaluate candidates
- Provide a comprehensive onboarding program to integrate new team members

By following this plan, we can assemble a highly skilled and collaborative team to effectively develop the Manus AI clone.
